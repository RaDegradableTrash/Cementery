# Bug Report

## Summary
**Title**: Very distant terrain is not visible until streamed chunks finish loading
**ID**: BUG-0003
**Severity**: S2-Major
**Priority**: P1-Immediate
**Status**: Open
**Reported**: 2026-07-08
**Reporter**: Player feedback

## Classification
- **Category**: Visual
- **System**: World streaming / distant terrain rendering
- **Frequency**: Always when looking beyond the currently loaded chunk range
- **Regression**: Unknown

## Environment
- **Build**: Unity 2022.3.62f3c1, WebGL target
- **Platform**: macOS editor play mode, WebGL target
- **Scene/Level**: Main_Persistent with streamed Desert_Chunk scenes
- **Game State**: Runtime grid streaming enabled

## Reproduction Steps
**Preconditions**: `WorldStreamer.useGridStreaming` is enabled and the player is in the desert world.

1. Enter play mode in `Main_Persistent`.
2. Rotate the camera toward terrain beyond the active streamed chunk range.
3. Move or look across the horizon before the far additive chunk scenes finish loading.

**Expected Result**: Very distant terrain remains visible immediately as a stable far landscape while detailed streamed chunks load in closer to the player.
**Actual Result**: Terrain beyond the loaded chunk range is missing or appears only after distant chunks finish loading, causing the visible world range to feel too short.

## Technical Context
- **Likely affected files**:
  - `Assets/Scripts/Environment/WorldStreamer.cs`
  - `Assets/Scripts/Environment/DesertTerrainChunk.cs`
- **Related systems**: Camera far clip, additive chunk loading, terrain material assignment, saved desert chunk meshes
- **Possible root cause**: The renderer depended entirely on additive chunk scene activation for far terrain visibility. Increasing the real chunk load radius alone risks WebGL load spikes and still leaves a visible gap while scenes activate.

## Evidence
- **Logs**: No error log required; issue is visible in runtime camera view.
- **Visual**: Player reports the far terrain range remains too low and distant terrain is not available in real time.

## Related Issues
- BUG-0001

## Notes
Fix should provide a low-cost distant terrain layer or equivalent far landscape representation while preserving detailed streamed chunks for near and mid range.
